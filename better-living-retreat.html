About the retreat

A Complete Reset for Body, Mind, and Rhythm

Modern life is fast, fragmented, and exhausting. The Better Living Retreat at Mindful Shapes Yoga was created for people who know they need a pause — not just a weekend, but a genuine immersive reset that rebuilds their relationship with their own body and daily rhythm.

This is an ashram-based residential retreat rooted in the traditional yogic lifestyle. Each day has a clear structure: early morning practice, meditation, meals taken in silence, afternoon teachings, evening reflection, and early sleep. The routine itself is the medicine.

Amogh Shinde personally leads key sessions throughout the retreat. The curriculum moves from opening and releasing in the first days, to deepening and integrating through the middle, to building a personalised home practice by the final days — so you leave not just rested, but equipped.

Daily Retreat Schedule

5:45 AM — Wake & Silence A gentle wake bell. No screens, no conversation. Personal journalling or walking in the ashram garden before practice.
6:15 AM — Morning Sadhana 75-min guided yoga practice: pranayama, meditation, and asana in a progressive sequence suited to retreat depth and duration.
7:45 AM — Sattvik Breakfast Fresh, vegetarian ashram breakfast taken mindfully. No discussions about work or digital life encouraged during the first week.
9:00–12:00 — Morning Teaching Theory sessions — yoga philosophy, anatomy, pranayama science, lifestyle and diet, or specific themed workshop based on retreat days.
12:30 PM — Lunch & Rest Main meal of the day followed by 90 min of free time for rest, reading, journalling, or quiet walks.
4:30 PM — Afternoon Practice Restorative yoga, yin, or therapeutic practice — slower-paced, focused on release, opening, and nervous system settling.
6:30 PM — Satsang / Discussion Evening group gathering — reflection, group sharing, chanting, or Q&A with Amogh Shinde. Light dinner follows.
9:00 PM — Rest Lights out, no devices. Early sleep is part of the retreat design — it allows deep early morning practice and natural rhythm restoration.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior yoga experience to attend the retreat?

No prior experience is needed. The retreat is designed to accommodate complete beginners as well as experienced practitioners. Sessions are adjusted by the instructor to meet everyone where they are. Many of our most transformative retreat participants were beginners who had never stepped on a yoga mat before.

Is accommodation included in the price?

Yes. All retreat prices include residential accommodation on the ashram campus. The base pricing is for shared rooms (2 people). Private room upgrades are available at an additional ₹1,500–2,500 per night depending on room availability. Please mention your preference when booking.

What is the food like? Can dietary restrictions be accommodated?

All meals are freshly prepared sattvik vegetarian food — free from onion, garlic, processed ingredients, and excessive spices. This is an intentional part of the retreat. Most common dietary needs (gluten-free, dairy-free, nut allergies) can be accommodated with advance notice. Please inform us during enrollment.

Are phones and internet allowed during the retreat?

We strongly encourage a digital detox, especially for the first 3–5 days. The ashram has WiFi available in the common area for essential use. We ask that phones be kept on silent and away from practice spaces. For 21-day participants, we recommend a complete first-week digital fast — the effect on practice depth is significant.

What happens if I need to leave early?

We understand life is unpredictable. If you need to leave before your retreat ends, we offer a partial refund based on the remaining unused days (after day 3). No refund is provided for departures within the first 3 days. Please see our full cancellation policy on the purchase page.
