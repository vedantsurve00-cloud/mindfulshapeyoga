About this program

Bringing Yoga to Where People Are — Not Just Where Yogis Go

Most people who need yoga the most — stressed office workers, teenagers with exam anxiety, communities recovering from hardship — never make it to a yoga studio. Our Camps and Wellness Workshops program exists to close that gap.

Mindful Shapes Yoga designs and delivers custom wellness programs for organisations, schools, residential societies, hospitals, NGOs, and events. Each program is built around your group's specific needs — the right intensity, duration, language (English, Marathi, Hindi, Spanish & French), and focus — so that it actually creates value rather than checking a wellness box.

We travel to your location anywhere in Pune, Mumbai, and across Maharashtra. We bring all equipment, mats, and instructors. You provide a suitable space — the rest is handled by us.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

We are a company in Mumbai. Can you come to our office?

Yes. We travel to Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Nagpur, and across Maharashtra for corporate and event sessions. Based in Chiplun, Pedhambee — travel and accommodation charges apply for locations outside Chiplun. WhatsApp us for a location-specific quote. We have conducted sessions in Mumbai offices for teams ranging from 10 to 200 people.

Our employees have varying fitness levels — will this work for everyone?

Absolutely. Our corporate sessions are specifically designed for mixed groups — we use chair yoga, gentle stretches, and modifications that work for anyone regardless of age, fitness, or flexibility. We never ask anyone to do something beyond their comfort level. The emphasis is on participation, not performance.

How much space do we need for a corporate session?

For a standard 30-person session, a clear floor space of approximately 20x30 feet is sufficient. We can also work in conference rooms, terraces, lawns, or any open space. We will assess the space during our pre-session consultation and advise on the best setup.

Can we book regular monthly sessions on a retainer?

Yes — and this is strongly recommended. One-off sessions raise awareness; monthly programs create lasting wellbeing habits. Our corporate retainer packages (4 sessions per month) are priced at a 20% discount versus individual sessions. Several Pune organisations have been on our monthly program for over a year.

Do you provide a certificate for employee participation?

Yes. For ongoing corporate wellness programs and wellness day events, we can issue branded participation certificates for each employee who completes the program. These work well as HR wellness incentives and are well received by participants.
