Amogh Shinde is the Founder of Mindful Shapes Yoga and a dedicated Yoga Guru who has devoted his life to preserving and sharing the timeless wisdom of Sanātana Dharma through the authentic science of Yoga. For over 12 years, he has guided students on a transformative journey toward physical well-being, mental clarity, and spiritual awakening.

His academic and traditional training includes a 900-hour Yoga Alliance Certification, a 200-hour Yoga Alliance Certification, and a 1600-hour Yoga Master's Certification under the Ministry of AYUSH, reflecting years of disciplined study and practice rooted in the classical yogic tradition.

After years of teaching and learning, Amogh made the conscious decision to return to his native town of Chiplun, Maharashtra, with a heartfelt mission — to make authentic yoga accessible to his community and inspire people to embrace yoga not merely as exercise, but as a complete way of life. Through Mindful Shapes Yoga, he strives to revive the ancient guru-śiṣya tradition while presenting yoga in a way that is practical, relevant, and transformative for modern society.

His teachings are deeply inspired by the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Bhagavad Gītā, the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, and the classical texts of Hatha Yoga. Every class integrates āsana, prāṇāyāma, dhyāna, philosophy, and mindful living, encouraging students to discover balance within themselves.

समत्वं योग उच्यते "Yoga is the state of inner equanimity." — Bhagavad Gītā 2.48

Amogh believes that the true purpose of yoga is not perfection of posture, but the awakening of consciousness. His vision is to help individuals cultivate discipline, compassion, self-awareness, and inner freedom through the eternal teachings of Yoga.

सा विद्या या विमुक्तये "True knowledge is that which leads to liberation."

With humility, dedication, and reverence for the ancient yogic lineage, Amogh continues to inspire students to walk the path of self-discovery, health, and higher consciousness — one breath, one practice, and one life at a time.
