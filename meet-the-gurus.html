Why Study With Us

What Every Student Can Expect

Two different teachers, one shared commitment to authentic, disciplined instruction.

Traditional Lineage

Teaching rooted in the guru-śiṣya tradition, drawing on the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gītā and the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali.

Formally Certified

Yoga Alliance and Ministry of AYUSH credentials, including a 1600-hour Yoga Master's Certification.

Holistic Practice

Every class blends āsana, prāṇāyāma, dhyāna and philosophy — never postures alone.

On Campus & Online

Taught from our ashram in Chiplun, Maharashtra, and available to students anywhere online.
