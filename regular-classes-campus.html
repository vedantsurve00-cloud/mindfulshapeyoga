About This Program

Yoga That Builds Real, Lasting Habit

At Mindful Shapes Yoga, regular classes are not just workout sessions — they are a systematic, teacher-guided journey toward stronger body awareness, deeper breath, and genuine postural transformation. Led personally by Amogh Shinde and his trained team, each class follows a structured progression that respects where you are today and builds toward where you want to be.

Whether you are stepping on a mat for the first time, returning after a long gap, or maintaining a steady practice — our regular classes create the consistent structure that makes real change possible.

What You Learn

A Progressive, Month-by-Month Curriculum

Each month builds on the last. Classes are never random — they follow a structured curriculum designed to deepen your practice systematically.

Month-by-Month Learning Path

Month 1 — Foundation Body awareness, breath basics, sun salutations, fundamental standing poses, safe spinal movement
Month 2 — Strength Core engagement, warrior sequences, balance poses, hip openers, introductory backbends
Month 3 — Breath Pranayama integration, Ujjayi breath, deeper forward folds, seated postures, concentration
Month 4+ — Deepening Inversions, advanced sequences, meditation introduction, advanced pranayama, daily practice design

Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Do I need prior yoga experience to join?

No experience is required. Our regular classes are designed to accommodate complete beginners. The instructor will offer modifications for every pose so you can practice safely from day one.

Can I attend both morning and evening batches?

The 24-class and 60-class packages include access to both batches. The 12-class package includes access to one fixed batch. You can switch batches once per month if space allows.

Where is the campus located and is parking available?

Our ashram campus is located in Pedhambee, Chiplun, Maharashtra. Two-wheeler and car parking is available on-site. Message us on WhatsApp for exact directions and a map link.

What do I need to bring for on-campus classes?

A yoga mat (we have a few spares if needed), comfortable clothing that allows movement, a small towel, and a water bottle. No footwear is required inside the yoga hall.

Can I pause my membership due to travel or illness?

Yes. We offer a pause option of up to 15 days per 3-month period. Unused days are carried forward. Please inform us in advance via WhatsApp or phone.

Is this class suitable for seniors or people with injuries?

We welcome students of all ages including seniors. Please inform the instructor about any injuries, surgeries, or health conditions before joining so that appropriate modifications can be prepared for you.
