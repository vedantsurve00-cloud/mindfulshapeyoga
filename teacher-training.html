About This Program

More Than a Certificate. A Complete Transformation.

The Mindful Shapes Yoga Teacher Training programs are not mass-enrollment courses designed to get you a certificate quickly. They are carefully structured journeys into the depth of yoga — covering asana, pranayama, human anatomy, yoga philosophy, Sanskrit basics, sequencing methodology, and supervised practice teaching under the guidance of Amogh Shinde.

You will graduate not just certified — but genuinely prepared to teach with confidence, safety, and authentic knowledge. Our graduates have gone on to teach independently, run their own studios, and join corporate wellness programs across Maharashtra and beyond.

Curriculum Overview

What You Study — Module by Module

200H Foundation Curriculum

Module 1 — Asana Science 60+ classical postures with alignment, modifications, contraindications, and sequencing principles
Module 2 — Pranayama 8 core pranayama techniques — Ujjayi, Kapalabhati, Nadi Shodhana, Bhramari, Bhastrika & more
Module 3 — Yoga Philosophy Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, 8 limbs of yoga (Ashtanga), Bhagavad Gita excerpts, Samkhya basics
Module 4 — Human Anatomy Skeletal structure, muscular groups, joint mechanics, nervous system, respiratory system as they relate to yoga practice
Module 5 — Teaching Methodology Class structure, cueing language, managing mixed levels, hands-on adjustments, demo techniques
Module 6 — Practice Teaching Supervised teaching sessions, peer feedback rounds, mentored evaluation, final assessment class

500H Advanced Additions (for graduates of 200H)

Advanced Studies

  • Advanced asana — arm balances, deep backbends, inversions
  • Yoga therapy fundamentals — chronic conditions, modifications
  • Mantra, mudra, and bandha science
  • Ayurveda basics for yoga practitioners
  • Advanced pranayama & Kumbhaka

Teaching Excellence

  • Advanced sequencing & theming
  • Teaching special populations (seniors, injuries)
  • Studio business fundamentals
  • Mentored teaching — 50+ supervised hours
  • Capstone project and final exam

Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

Is prior yoga teaching experience required?

No. You only need a minimum of 6 months of regular personal yoga practice. No prior teaching experience is needed — the program teaches you everything from the ground up.

Is the 200H certification internationally recognized?

Our curriculum is structured to international standards of yoga teacher training. Upon graduation, you will receive a certificate from Mindful Shapes Yoga under Amogh Shinde. We recommend checking registration requirements with your local yoga association if you plan to teach abroad.

Can I do the 500H program without completing 200H here?

If you have a valid 200H certificate from another accredited institution, you may be eligible to join the 500H track directly. Please contact us with your certificate details for evaluation.

What language is the training conducted in?

Training is conducted in English, Marathi, Hindi, Spanish and French. Study materials are primarily in English. Marathi, Hindi, Spanish or French-medium batches may be available on request depending on group size.

Are payment installments available?

Yes. We offer a 2-installment payment plan: 60% at enrollment and 40% on the first day of the program. Contact us to discuss flexible payment options if needed.

What happens after I graduate?

You receive your certificate, join our alumni network, and get 3 months of post-graduation support including access to Amogh Shinde for teaching questions. Many graduates also assist with camps, and workshops at the academy.
